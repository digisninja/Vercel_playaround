The journey

From uncertain to confident, one step at a time

Most people stop moving well because they stop trusting their body. The path back runs in this order: learn how it works, measure where you are, then train with a reason behind every move.

How it connects

Every joint has a job

Your joints alternate down the body: one built to move, the next built to stay steady. When a mobile joint gets stiff, its stable neighbour is forced to move instead, and that is often where the pain shows up. This is the joint-by-joint idea used by many physical therapists and coaches.

Built to move (mobility)Built to hold steady (stability)
Step 04 · Train

A week built around your weakest links

Mobility on three days, strength on two, and a little balance every day. Short sessions you can repeat matter more than long ones you skip.
